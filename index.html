How much does an employee really cost?

Most first-time employers budget the salary and forget everything stacked on top of it. Between employer payroll taxes, unemployment insurance, benefits, and workers' comp, a single employee typically costs 25% to 40% more than their salary. A $50,000 hire often costs $62,000 to $70,000 a year. Use the calculator above with your own numbers before you make an offer.

What is included in employer payroll costs

  • Social Security (6.2%): you match your employee's contribution, up to the annual IRS wage base.
  • Medicare (1.45%): you match this on all wages, with no cap.
  • FUTA (0.6%): federal unemployment tax on the first $7,000 of each employee's wages.
  • SUTA: state unemployment tax. Rates and wage bases vary by state; new employers usually pay 2–4%.
  • Benefits: health insurance, retirement matches, and paid time off are often the biggest add-on.
  • Workers' comp: required in nearly every state; rates depend on how risky the work is.

Frequently asked questions

Is this payroll calculator free?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser. No account, no signup, nothing uploaded anywhere.

Does this include the employee's income tax?

No. This calculator shows the employer's cost: salary plus the taxes and expenses you pay on top of it. Income tax comes out of the employee's paycheck, not your pocket.

How accurate are the tax rates?

Federal rates (Social Security, Medicare, FUTA) are fixed by law. State unemployment and workers' comp vary widely — adjust those two fields to match your state for a sharper number.

Why does an employee cost more than their salary?

Because the salary is only the starting point. Employer payroll taxes add about 7.65% on FICA alone, unemployment insurance adds more, and benefits plus workers' comp can add thousands per person per year.

Should I do payroll myself or use a service?

One employee in one state is manageable by hand. The moment you add a second state, contractors, or benefits, a payroll platform usually pays for itself in time saved and penalties avoided. The options above cover everything from small business payroll to global teams.
